3 Instagram settings worth changing today 👇

1️⃣ Hide like counts
Settings and activity → Like and share counts → turn on Hide like & share counts. Scroll without the numbers game.

2️⃣ Turn on Quiet mode
Settings and activity → Notifications → Quiet mode. Mute notifications and auto-reply to DMs while you focus or sleep, and schedule it for any hours.

3️⃣ Hide when you're online
Settings and activity → Messages and story replies → Show activity status → switch it off. Nobody sees when you're online (and you won't see theirs either).
