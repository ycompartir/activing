3 lessons from Marcus Aurelius for overthinkers 🏛️

I. "The soul becomes dyed with the color of its thoughts." — Meditations 5.16
II. "Never let the future disturb you. You will meet it, if you have to, with the same weapons of reason which today arm you against the present." — Meditations 7.8
III. "Very little is needed to make a happy life; it is all within yourself, in your way of thinking." — Meditations 7.67

Which one does your mind need today? I, II or III 👇
Save this for the next time you can't switch off. 🤍
