DESCRIPTION (819/5000)
3 iPhone Camera tricks that instantly level up your photos 👇

1️⃣ Turn on the grid
Settings → Camera → turn on Grid. Use the lines to keep horizons straight and place your subject on the rule of thirds.

2️⃣ Lock focus & exposure
In the Camera app, press and hold on your subject until you see AE/AF LOCK. Then drag the sun icon up or down to set the brightness. Focus and exposure stay locked even if you move.

3️⃣ Shoot in burst mode
Slide the shutter button to the left and hold. Your iPhone fires off dozens of shots in seconds, perfect for kids, pets and sports. Keep the sharpest one.
