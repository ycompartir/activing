DESCRIPTION (762/5000)
3 Stoic rules to carry you through a hard day.

I. "We suffer more often in imagination than in reality." — Seneca
Most of the pain is in the story we tell ourselves about what might happen.

II. "Make the best use of what is in your power, and take the rest as it happens." — Epictetus
Focus on your effort, your choices, your response. Release the rest.

III. "What stands in the way becomes the way." — Marcus Aurelius
The obstacle isn't blocking your path. It is the path.

Which one do you need today? Comment I, II or III.
