3 Google Maps tricks you'll actually use every week 👇

1️⃣ Use maps with no signal
Tap your profile picture → Offline maps → Select your own map → Download. Search and get driving directions even with zero signal or in airplane mode. Perfect for road trips and travel abroad.

2️⃣ Save your parking spot
Tap the blue dot → Save parking. A pin marks exactly where you left the car. Add a note like "Level 3, spot B12" so you never wander a parking garage again.

3️⃣ Share your live location
Tap the blue dot → Share location → pick a contact and how long. They see you move in real time until the timer runs out.
