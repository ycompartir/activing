DESCRIPTION (767/5000)
3 Gmail tricks that make your inbox way less stressful 👇

1️⃣ Schedule send
Writing late at night? Tap the 3-dot menu → Schedule send → pick a time. Your email lands at 8 AM, not at midnight.

2️⃣ Swipe to archive
Swipe any email in your inbox to archive it in one move. Want a different action? Go to Settings → Swipe actions and set it to delete, snooze, mark as read and more.

3️⃣ Snooze an email
Open it, tap the 3-dot menu → Snooze → choose when. The email disappears now and comes back to the top of your inbox exactly when you need it.
