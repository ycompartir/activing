3 Spotify tricks most listeners never find 👇

1️⃣ Set a sleep timer
On the song that's playing, tap the 3-dot menu → Sleep timer and pick how long. The music stops on its own while you fall asleep.

2️⃣ Swipe to add to queue
In any playlist or album, swipe a song to the right to add it to your queue. It plays next without changing your playlist.

3️⃣ Try Smart Shuffle
Tap the shuffle button twice to turn on Smart Shuffle. Spotify mixes in new songs that match the vibe of your playlist (Spotify Premium).
