3 YouTube tricks that make watching way better 👇

1️⃣ Double-tap to skip
Double-tap the right side of the video to skip ahead 10 seconds, or the left side to go back. Keep tapping to jump further.

2️⃣ Hold for 2x speed
Press and hold anywhere on the video to play it at 2x speed. Let go and it returns to normal. Great for slow intros.

3️⃣ Set a sleep timer
Tap the video → Settings → Sleep timer and pick how long. The video pauses on its own, perfect for falling asleep to a video.
