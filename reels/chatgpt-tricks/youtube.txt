DESCRIPTION (872/5000)
3 ChatGPT tricks that instantly give you better answers 👇

1️⃣ Make it ask you first
End your prompt with "Before answering, ask me 3 questions." ChatGPT gathers the context it needs, so the final answer actually fits you.

2️⃣ Give it a role + format
Start with "Act as a…" (travel planner, editor, coach) and say exactly how you want the answer: a list, a table, 3 bullet points. Role + format = answers you can use right away.

3️⃣ Set custom instructions
Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions. Tell ChatGPT once about your job, tone and favorite format, and it applies to every new chat.
